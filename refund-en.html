Refund & Cancellation Policy

When and how you can cancel and get a refund.

Trial membership

The trial is completely free, needs no cancellation and ends automatically.

Requesting a refund

You can request a full refund within 7 days of payment, provided you have not used any benefit, coupon or service of the paid membership.

Non-refundable cases

No refund is given more than 7 days after payment or after any membership benefit has been used, unless we could not provide the service or the law requires otherwise.

Duplicate or incorrect charges

If you were charged twice or the wrong amount, contact us and we will return the difference in full after checking.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 14 business days of approval. Your bank may take longer to show them.

Cancelling

You can ask to cancel your membership at any time. It stays active until the end of its term unless it was refunded.
