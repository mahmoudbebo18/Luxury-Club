About Us

DASMAN is an exclusive membership within The Luxury Club, designed to give its members a complete experience that brings services, privileges and exclusive offers together in one platform.

Our Message

Provision of high-quality solutions and services to provide our customers with the best deals and services.

Our Vision

  • Seeking the increase of services by concluding agreements and building partnerships with distinguished service providers.
  • Reaching leadership and becoming the first choice in Saudi Arabia.
  • Working to attract the best international companies as partners in order to provide the best offers and rewards to subscribers.
  • Hiring the best highly skilled staff to provide the best quality services to subscribers.

DASMAN Services

With a DASMAN membership, members get an integrated set of services designed to save time, effort and money. Highlights include:

Legal Services

Lawyers and a legal team for consultations, contract drafting and case follow-up.

Logistics Services

Airport services, item delivery, shipping, and pickup and drop-off of orders and belongings.

Offers & Rewards

Exclusive discounts and rewards at partner stores, hotels, restaurants and events.

Concierge

Restaurant and event bookings, plus a personal assistant for your requests and daily tasks.

Scientific Workshops

Regular workshops in photography and scientific research to build your academic and practical skills.

Roadside Assistance

Towing, tire changes, battery charging and fuel delivery if your car breaks down.

Recreational Activities

Regular padel and football tournaments and fun activities that bring DASMAN members together.
